ARCANE RPG B04 — Mad Maze enemies

72 horizontal strips across six enemies and three views. This roster starts with five Bramble encounter enemies plus the Mothling minion. Asset names use the enemy IDs from game/shared/mm-enemies.js.

Each 256 × 256 cell uses a 4 × 4 pixel grid, binary transparency, a 12 px minimum margin, and ground anchor (128, 231). Left mirrors right. Each enemy has four idle, six move, five attack, and five death frames per direction. Separate effects and projectiles belong in the game layer.

Original image-generated source sheets are cropped and palette-quantized at a fixed scale per sheet. The movement and battle sheets are scale-matched using their standing poses to preserve natural changes within each animation. Intake passed with zero blockers and zero warnings.
