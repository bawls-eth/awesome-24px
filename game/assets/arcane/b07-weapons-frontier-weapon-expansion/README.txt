ARCANE RPG — Frontier weapon expansion

Twenty original 32 × 32 static weapon icons extend the eight weapon icons
in the earlier Frontier equipment pack to 28. IDs, tiers, and tags are
proposed catalog metadata. The game can assign stats independently.

Sprites use RGBA, binary alpha, the shared B04 palette, and transparent
padding. Each silhouette spans 24–26 pixels on its longest side.
