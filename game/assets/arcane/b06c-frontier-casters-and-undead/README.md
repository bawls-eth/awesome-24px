# B06c Frontier Casters and Undead

72 original animation strips and 360 frames for six existing Arcane Frontier enemy IDs: Ember Imp, Ice Wraith, Goblin Shaman, Skeleton Archer, Bone Knight, and Shadow Cultist. Each down, up and right view has four idle, six move, five attack and five death frames. Left mirrors right.

Cells are 256 × 256, with a (128, 231) anchor, binary alpha and the shared enemy palette. Attack muzzle points are provisional and should be checked in-game. The contact sheet shows all frames.

Shadow Cultist's combat hood shows small eyes and its belt detail differs slightly from the movement poses. Compare the transition at gameplay scale during integration.
