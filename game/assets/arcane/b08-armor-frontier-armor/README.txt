ARCANE RPG — B08 Frontier armor

50 original 32 × 32 inventory icons. Nine coordinated armor themes have
cuirasses, jerkins, pauldrons, gauntlets and greaves. Five themes also
have boots. In preview.png, the six rows follow that order.

Each item has binary transparency, a palette from the earlier packs,
at least three pixels of padding and a 24–26 px longest silhouette
dimension. Paired gear is shown together as a single inventory icon.
The IDs, tiers and tags are proposed catalog metadata.
