          What is a Fall?

          A Fall is what happens when a hero runs out of HP in the arena. It is the only loss in Mad Maze, and it only happens in Classic mode.

          • The hero is lost. That hero steps into the Hall of Heroes.
          • Your fame is banked. The fame the hero earned is added to your account for good.
          • A Keepsake goes to your stash. One memento to remember them by.
          • Picnic mode never falls. At 0 HP a Picnic hero just comes back here with everything.

          After a Fall you can Rekindle a new hero of the same class at half the level, up to level 10. Heroes at level 20 can instead Retire to the Hall with their full fame, no Fall needed.
