Pixel Pop

Tap 2 or more touching twins. They pop into one tile a level higher, right where you tapped.

If the new tile lands next to its twin, it pops again: a chain. Chains multiply your points.

Pop 5 at once for a Big pop (+2 levels). Each new best tile from level 5 adds 5 seconds back. 90 seconds a round.

    Time! 0 points

      Keys: arrows move, Enter or Space pops. Free to play: no timers outside a round, nothing to buy.
