Stack Tower

Tap to drop the swinging block onto the tower. The part that hangs over the edge falls off.

Land it right on top for a Perfect. Three Perfects in a row make the block wider again.

The swing speeds up as you climb. Miss the tower completely and the round ends. 90 seconds at most.
