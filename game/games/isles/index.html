    How to play

    1. Move: stick or WASD / arrows.
    2. Attack: the big button, Space or click.
    3. Walk over sparkles to gather; Gather at spots.
    4. Soothe a creature to sleep, then Feed it to tame.
    5. Pack: party, craft, log and help. Sail at docks.

    Keys: WASD / arrows move. Space / Enter fires. F, Shift or E feeds (the mount ability while riding). Esc / P menu. M map, Q soothe, R rides, C party command. G gathers, K crafts, T sails at a dock, L log, B decor at home, P trophies at home.
