How to play

Walk close, then press Space or tap Swing to net a bug. Sneak (Shift) so bugs do not flee. Bugs hide in flowers, burrow in mounds when you move, and swarms scatter together. Stand still near a mound to lure the Antlion out. In the Night Glade, sneak up on fireflies slowly.

WASD / arrows walk. Hold Shift or E to sneak. Space, Enter or click swings the net. Esc or P pauses.
