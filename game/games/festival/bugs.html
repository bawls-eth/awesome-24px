How to play

Walk close, then press Space or tap Swing to net a bug. Sneak (Shift) so bugs do not flee. Bugs hide in flowers, burrow in mounds when you move, and swarms scatter together. Stand still near a mound to lure the Antlion out.

WASD / arrows walk. Hold Shift to sneak. Space or click swings the net.
