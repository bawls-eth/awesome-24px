Echo Garden

Watch and listen: the flowers sing a little tune, one after another.

Then tap them back in the same order. Each tune you echo grows one note longer.

A wrong flower wilts a leaf and the tune plays again, slower. Three leaves a round, up to 2 minutes.

    Round over: 0

      Keys: 1 to 4 tap a flower, or arrows move and Enter or Space taps. Free to play: nothing to buy.
