forgotten. Sections: `## Proven fixes` (all prompts), `## class: <id>` (one class), `## action: <name>` (one action).
Keep each bullet one short imperative sentence. Order is preserved (deterministic prompts).

## Proven fixes
- Both legs and both boots are fully visible in every frame (no leg hidden behind a cape, robe or shield).
- No weapon trail, no swoosh, no slash arc: draw only the weapon itself.
- No motion blur, no speed lines, no smear frames.
- The back view shows no face at all: no eyes, no mouth, no profile.
- The side view is a true profile: exactly 90 degrees, near arm and near leg in front of the torso.
