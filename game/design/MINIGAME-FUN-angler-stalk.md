# Mini game fun notes: Angler + Stalk Market (research, 10 lines)
1. Fishing games hook players on the tension loop: wait, sudden bite cue, tight reaction window, then a skill-based reel; the bite cue must be loud (sound + flash) so misses feel fair.
2. Near-miss feedback ("too early by 0.3 s", "the catch meter was at 92%") turns a fail into "one more try" instead of frustration.
3. Streaks for consecutive catches give a short-term goal inside an endless loop; they reset gently and never punish.
4. Personal bests (best streak, best practice profit) give a self-competition chase with no pressure or timers.
5. Collection goals (journal 0/48, cove set) are the long-term pull; "New!" call-outs make each catch matter.
6. Juice: short screen shake on hook/land, particle burst scaled by rarity, distinct sfx per outcome; all respect reduced-motion and low-gfx.
7. Trading games are fun when decisions are informed bets: a clear chart with the buy line, honest pattern odds, and a live "if you sell now" profit line.
8. Real-time weekly markets have long dead time; a seeded, instant "practice week" gives short rounds and instant restarts without touching the real save.
9. An end-of-round summary (result, best, what the week's pattern was) plus one-tap Play again keeps rounds short and satisfying.
10. No dark patterns: no fake urgency, no streak guilt, no paid randomness; the odds shown are the real model odds and bells are play money.
