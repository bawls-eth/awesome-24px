# New mini games: what makes them fun (Pixel Pop, Lantern Dash)

1. Short rounds (Pixel Pop: 90 s; Lantern Dash: usually 30-120 s) with one-tap Play again. Losing is cheap, so you try again for fun, not out of pressure.
2. Juice is feedback: pops, particles, floating score text, a small shake and a sound on every success. All of it respects reduced motion (no shake or blinking) and the Low graphics pref (fewer particles, no glow).
3. Combos reward skill, not time spent. Pop: chains come from planning where the merged tile lands. Dash: Perfects come from jumping late and on purpose.
4. Gentle first 30 seconds: a three-line how-to on the first visit only. Dash starts slow with three small rocks. A bad tap in Pop costs nothing and the game says why.
5. A clear skill curve: Dash speeds up, gaps open, and twin rocks appear (hold to clear both). Pop adds higher tiles as you climb, and Big pops (5+) plus time bonuses reward planning ahead.
6. Seeded variety: every board or course comes from a seed (MMRng). "Replay seed" / "Play again" gives the same layout so you can master it; "New course" / a new round gives a fresh one.
7. Ghost of your best: Dash replays your best run's inputs on the same course (deterministic 60 Hz sim) and puts a "best" flag at your record distance.
8. Personal best plus four simple medals per game, with the next goal on screen. Medals go to the Hub via `pixeltown-medals-<game>-v1`.
9. No dark patterns: no streaks, no logins, no daily gates, no fake urgency, no money, no loot boxes. Missing a goal costs nothing.
10. Phone-first, one thumb: board cells about 70 px, a full-width 72 px Jump button, every button at least 44 px. Desktop keys: arrows and Enter, or Space.
