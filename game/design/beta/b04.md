# Beta b04: Realm world (maps, collisions, zones, Nexus, NPCs, quests)

Tester b04, branch `beta-b04` from `claude/animal-crossing-game-7z3roh` @ ae30c26, local server on port 9453. Database blocked in every context (`blockDb`). I sent no chat and wrote nothing to Firebase.
Time: about 60 min (05:26-06:00 UTC). The machine was heavily loaded (load average about 23), so several sessions waited for a slot.

## What I covered
- **Fresh start (desktop, no save)**: hero picker, coach, HUD, first walk with real WASD, M / K keys, minimap click (big map), Esc menu, restart confirm.
- **Terrain tour (seed 12345, Lv 12)**: one screenshot on every terrain type: shallow / wet sand / sand / grass / tall grass / dirt and stone road / dark grass / flower meadow / dry grass / cracked stone / highland rock / gravel / snow / godland ash / blood ruins. I took each screenshot right after arriving and again 0.9 s later, to look for chunk pop-in.
- **Collision (real keys)**: walked into a cracked-stone wall, a deep-water shore, a concave pocket, 8 prop kinds (altar, 4 portals, campfire, tent, ruin pillar) and the west and north map edges, then slid diagonally and backed off each time. Walls and deep water block cleanly. **I never got stuck.** Map edges and the camera clamp work.
- **Shallow water**: 0.7x speed confirmed (`moveScale` 0.7, about 155 vs 208 px/s by real key hold).
- **Zone ladder (Lv 1)**: walked across the Shore→Midlands, Midlands→Highlands and Highlands→Godlands borders with real keys. Every crossing showed the correct toast with the recommended level and a 1 s 0.5x slow on the first crossing. Re-crossing does not slow again.
- **Nexus (desktop and phone)**: Visit button, walking with WASD into all four plaza corners, every NPC (Vendor, Blacksmith, Healer, Quest Giver, Guide, Oracle, Wanderer) with E and with the prompt button, the daily quest board, Escape behaviour, and the Realm portal return (back to exactly the same tile, 32,95).
- **Seeds and robustness**: `?seed=abc`, `?seed=zz` and the seed 687197 picked when none is given all make different worlds. An invalid seed (`<script>`) falls back to the saved seed. Hostile `?at=1,1`, `9999,9999` and `-5,3` are ignored safely, and the hero always lands on a free tile.
- **Phone 390x844 touch**: HUD, minimap tap, move stick drag (CDP touch), Nexus plaza and Vendor stall, rotation to 844x390 and back.
- Console / page errors: **none** in any session.

## Top findings
1. **b04-04 (P2)**: The daily quest says "Defeat 22 creatures in **the Deeps**", but no zone is called that. `mm-quests.js:8` names the zones Shore / **Meadows** / **Deeps** / Godlands, while the HUD, toasts and goal line say Shore / **Midlands** / **Highlands** / Godlands. A new player cannot find the quest zone.
2. **b04-01 (P2)**: The per-tile shade patches are hard-edged rectangles, so big grass, sand and highland areas look like a quilt of tile seams. Of everything in the overworld art, this does the most to make the ground look blocky and flat.
3. **b04-05 (P2)**: The Nexus plaza camera is not clamped. Black void bands show at the plaza edges (top/left in a corner, right/bottom near the Quest Giver), on desktop and on the phone.
4. **b04-06 (P2)**: On a phone, the move stick sits on top of the Vendor stall's Buy buttons, and the vendor line overflows its bubble.
5. **b04-02 (P2)**: The Godlands (Lethal) look almost the same as the Highlands: grey on grey with the same props. The end-game zone has no visual identity of its own.
6. **b04-07 (P2, confirms an open bugbash item)**: The phone Nexus plaza still uses only about 40% of the screen, and the portal labels are clipped.
7. **b04-08 (P3)**: Escape with the quest board or a dialog open leaves the whole Nexus. The quest board has no close button of its own.
8. **b04-09 (P3)**: The Blacksmith points you to the backpack Forge, then opens the "Vendor stall" with the vendor's shelf.
9. **b04-11 (P3)**: No key opens the overworld map. M and Tab do nothing, although the dungeon uses them. The fresh-island minimap is about 95% black, with a tiny player patch.
10. **b04-13 (P3, balance)**: A Lv 1 hero can walk straight into the Lethal Godlands with only a toast and a 1 s slow. Two world bosses (Frost Dragon, Shadow Hydra) sit about 10 tiles apart on the Highlands/Godlands border for seed 12345.

Also: b04-03 mixed edge styles (round snow blobs, dashed dry grass, square steps everywhere else); b04-10 desktop quest list below the fold; b04-12 big props (tents, towers, cabins) are walk-through; b04-14 the Nexus hero is a yellow dot, not the hero sprite.

## What felt great
- Movement and collision are solid: no snagging on wall corners, diagonal sliding along walls works, deep-water shores block without jitter, and the camera clamps cleanly at the world edge.
- The zone toasts plus the "Next goal" line give a clear direction ("Reach Lv 5 for Midlands"), and the 1 s slow on a first over-level crossing is a nice, gentle warning.
- The Nexus return lands on exactly the tile you left. The NPC lines are short and charming, and the quests are deterministic per date.
- Seeds really do give different islands, and hostile URL params are handled safely.
- No pop-in seen in any of the 16 teleports: the chunks were baked by the next frame.

## Readiness for my area: 7 / 10
The world plays correctly (collision, zones, portals, quests). What holds it back is presentation: blocky shade patches, a Godlands without its own look, the Nexus camera void, phone Nexus layout, and quest naming.
