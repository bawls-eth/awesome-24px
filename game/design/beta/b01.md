# Beta b01: Realm combat and enemy AI

Tester b01, branch `beta-b01`, 2026-11-23 05:26-06:05 UTC (~40 min of play, 13 scripted sessions). Database blocked in every context (route + routeWebSocket), no live traffic.
Findings: `b01.json` (0 P0, 0 P1, 3 P2, 7 P3). Screenshots: `img/b01-01..09.jpg`.

## What I covered
- **Desktop 1280x720, mouse aim + hold fire:** fresh hero from the picker (Thicket Ranger, Good-Dog Herald, the default hero), 70-80 s hunts on the Shore and across the Midlands border. Lv 1 to 4 in ~80 s. Q (Dash) and E (Nova) used every few seconds, R and F checked while locked.
- **Phone 390x844 touch:** twin sticks driven with real CDP touch events (move plus aim, aim assist on, Q tapped with a third finger), and **One-thumb mode** switched on through the real Settings sheet (gear button), playing with the move stick alone. Rotated to 844x390 landscape mid-fight.
- **Damage and i-frames:** every hit taken was hooked and logged. Shore bolts do 6, Midlands bolts 9, which matches MMDifficulty (`round(8 x 0.73..1.09)`). The shortest gap between two hits was 0.73 s (0.5 s i-frames work). Damage numbers and the "-6" popups are readable.
- **Death and respawn, 5 times:** the fall card appears about 2.5-3 s after 0 hp. Try again puts you at the world spawn with full hp and 1.4 s of i-frames, and the nearest enemy is about 640 px away. Standing still for 4 s after a respawn cost no hp, so there is no spawn camping. Pressing R before the card appears does nothing (it works once the card is up).
- **Ability spam:** 20 Q presses in 1 s gave exactly one dash (cooldown respected). E, R and F show "Lv3/Lv5/Lv7" and do nothing while locked. No console errors.
- **Enemy behaviour:**
  - Whirler (anchored spiral), Sniper (orbits at 200-280 px with 3-bolt bursts) and Burster (closes to 150 px, 14-bolt ring).
  - Wake radius about 720 px. No leash (b01-05).
  - Hold-fire rule (b01-03).
  - Per-zone fire budget: 40, 80, 140 and 200 live bolts from Shore to Godlands.
  - Telegraph rings appear from the Highlands inward.
  - FPS stayed at 52-57 in every zone, even with 189 bolts in the Godlands.
- **Edges and corners:**
  - The world corner (0,0) is open sea: the hero is clamped and nothing breaks.
  - Diagonal "staircase" coastlines stop the hero dead when only one key is held (normal tile collision, not filed).
  - Phone oddity b01-10.
- **Overworld boss:** Sand Titan altar on seed 4242. It woke on a Lv 1 hero still inside the "Shore · Safe-ish" area. The hero died in 31 s while dealing 30% of the boss's hp (b01-02, b01-07).

## Top findings
1. **b01-01 (P2): the quest/goal line names zones that do not exist.** It tells a Lv 1-2 hero to "Defeat 22 creatures in the Deeps". `mm-quests.js` calls the zones Shore/Meadows/Deeps/Godlands, while the HUD, the toasts and mm-zones say Shore/Midlands/Highlands/Godlands. The goal line also prefers the daily quest over the level-appropriate hint, so a brand-new player is pointed at the Lv 10 ring.
2. **b01-02 (P2): a Midlands altar boss wakes on a Lv 1 hero standing in the Shore.** The wake check is a 9-tile radius with no zone check, and the zone toast says "recommended Lv 1" at the same moment.
3. **b01-03 (P2): hold-fire asymmetry.** The hero's bolts reach 497 px, but enemies more than 295 px above or below are "off-screen" and may not fire, so stationary Whirlers can be farmed out of view with no risk.
4. **b01-04 (P3): the Survivor's cache pays on every death, even with 0 kills.** Dying in a loop gives a free item each time (and +3 shards per death once the pack is full).
5. **b01-05 (P3): no leash or return-home, and enemies are 3-10x slower than the hero.** Kiting is trivial, and enemies left behind sleep wherever they stopped.
6. **b01-06 (P3): phone HUD text problems.** The goal line is cut off, the gear toast covers the zone and goal lines, and the F label shows "Blizzar".
7. **b01-07 (P3): the boss bar covers the boss near the top of the screen.** A zone banner also pops up mid-fight.
8. **b01-08 (P3): stick vectors are not cleared on blur or on a lost pointer.** In emulation, after a third-finger tap the hero kept running and firing with no fingers down. This is possibly partly an emulation artifact, but `onBlur` resets only the keys and the mouse.
9. **b01-09 (P3): enemies have no separation inland.** Wraith Knight and imp camps stack into one blob.
10. **b01-10 (P3, once): on the phone the hero ended up on a solid deep-water tile at the world edge.** It could not be reproduced on purpose. The "stuck" escape rule in `_movePlayer` would then allow free ocean walking.

## What felt great
- **One-thumb mode is excellent on a phone.** With only the move stick held, the hero auto-targeted 1700 times in 45 s, went from Lv 1 to 4, and never wasted a shot.
- **Desktop mouse aim + hold fire is tight.** Hits feel fair (6 dmg, 0.5 s i-frames) and the Shore is gentle for a new hero.
- **Death is painless.** The fall card is clear, the respawn is safe, and no shards are lost.
- **Zone danger scales visibly and readably.** It goes from a few Shore Snipers to Godlands bullet walls, and FPS holds at about 55 throughout.
- **Ability cooldowns and locked states are clear.** The Dash trail and the cooldown digits read well.

## Game-readiness (Realm combat + enemy AI): 7/10
The core loop is solid, with no crashes or console errors in 13 sessions. Before launch, fix the onboarding signposting (b01-01, b01-02) and the risk-free exploits (b01-03, b01-04).
