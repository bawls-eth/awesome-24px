# Beta tester b02: Realm loot, gear and menus

Branch `beta-b02`, port 9451, 2026-11-23 05:26-06:30 UTC. Findings: `b02.json` (15). Screenshots: `img/b02-01.jpg` to `img/b02-12.jpg`.

## What I covered (about 60 min)
- Realm world (`/game/games/mad-maze/index.html?mode=world&test=1&offline=1`; `test=1` only to read `window.__mm` state), seeded saves in localStorage (bruiser / ranger heroes, Lv 3 and Lv 20), DB blocked (blockDb + every WebSocket closed, 0 DB attempts, 0 console errors in every run).
- Backpack: Gear / Stats / Relics tabs (desktop 1280x720, phone 390x844 touch, rotate to 844x390), slot boxes, tier badges and shape badges, compare on hover (desktop) and on tap (phone), stats drop-down, chase line and relic book.
- Equip / unequip, combine 3 into the next rarity, sell, double clicks and double taps on Sell / Combine / Reforge confirm / Salvage confirm / Shop buy.
- Forge: salvage (confirm step), reforge with exactly enough dust and shards.
- Shop with a 23/24 pack (double tap to buy); Nexus vendor stall stock and buy-back text.
- Loot bags: dropped r0-r2 and T5 / T9 / T12 bags next to the player and walked into them; a full pack (24/24) with a T12 drop at Lv 20 and at Lv 3 (over-level).
- Real play: about 40 s of desktop WASD + Space + mouse aim in the Shore (xp and shards gained, no natural bag dropped in that time).
- Persistence: reload after sell / combine / reforge / buy; two tabs selling the same item; hostile save values (negative shards and dust, a duplicate uid, an out-of-range rarity).

## Coverage gaps (not reached)
- Natural boss and dungeon T12 drops, and sets (Banded / Dread pair) being activated in real play. The set rules were only read, not tested.
- The Nexus vendor stall was reached on phone with `?seed=12345` (teleport next to the Nexus, then talk to the Vendor): I tested double-tap buy and the Sell back confirm. On my first, unseeded try `nexus.open()` returned false. I did not reach it on desktop.
- Town-side Realm overlay, the stash, Lantern finds. I did not record clips; no finding needed a clip.

## Top findings
1. **b02-01 (P1)**: when you hold 3 or more copies of an item, the Combine button is drawn exactly on top of the Equip button. Both use `button.eq`, which is pinned to grid column 2, row 1. You cannot equip that item at all, on desktop or phone. Suspect `realm-ui.css:94` and `mad-maze.css:213`.
2. **b02-02 (P1)**: a normal double-click on Sell on desktop sells two items. After the list re-renders, the next row's Sell button is under the cursor. In my run this sold the equipped armor. Reproduced 2/2. A phone double-tap sold only one item.
3. **b02-03 (P1)**: Sell has no confirm step, even for the equipped T12 weapon or the relic-book Dread Plate. Salvage already asks first. A T12 sells for only 31 shards.
4. **b02-04 (P1)**: with a full pack, a T12 drop is auto-sold, including an over-level T12. The specs say otherwise: GEAR-DROPS-BUILD-PLAN.md:61 says over-level drops are never auto-sold, and NEXT-SLICES-3.md:119 allows "or the bag stays on the ground".
5. **b02-05 (P2)**: nothing warns you that the pack is full. The "sold X +N" text is faint green on green grass.
6. **b02-12 (P2)**: double-tapping Confirm reforge charges only once (good), but the second tap opens the Salvage confirm for the item you just forged.
7. **b02-08 (P2)**: the Stats tab keeps the red compare deltas of the last tapped item (-3.9 PWR, -6.3 LCK), which looks like a debuff. "Wearing: Spark Wand" is ambiguous when both items are Spark Wands.
8. **b02-06 (P2)**: loot bags are 10-14 px flat squares. A T12 bag is a small white square, not the red bag with sparkle from the spec. This may be waiting on Codex's b15 bag art.
9. **b02-07 / b02-09 (P2)**: the "New best tier" toast covers the backpack header. In phone landscape, the "Next goal" HUD label is drawn over the backpack card.
10. **b02-13 / b02-14 (P2)**: in the Nexus vendor stall on phone, the round touch pad button covers the Buy list. A double-tap on Buy buys twice, with no confirm. Sell back offers the equipped weapon without marking it as equipped.
11. **b02-10 (P3)**: gear rows have uneven, tall layouts. On desktop only about 1.5 items are visible at once, there is no sort, and new loot lands at the bottom of 24 rows.

## What felt great
- The 8-bit backpack card looks good: clear tabs, the four stat tiles with plain words ("How hard each shot hits"), a readable stats table with Total, Base and Gear columns, and a calm relic book ("Nothing here runs out").
- The forge is honest: exact costs shown before you pay, a confirm step for salvage, and a reforge double-tap charges only once.
- Saves hold up. Two tabs selling the same item pay only once. Hostile values are cleaned (negative shards and dust become 0, duplicate uids are dropped). Every sell, combine, reforge and buy survives a reload. The shop refuses a second buy into a full pack.
- No page errors or console errors and 0 DB traffic in every run.

## Readiness score for my area: 5/10
The core systems are solid and well guarded at the data level. The UI layer has two easy-to-hit ways to lose valuable gear: the double-click sell and Sell with no confirm. Pickup also silently auto-sells T12 chase drops when the pack is full, and items held in triples cannot be equipped. Fix b02-01 to b02-04 and this area is about an 8.

## What I could NOT test and why
- Natural boss and godland drops (time; the bag chance in the Shore is low). I simulated drops with `arena.dropBag` at the player's feet and picked them up by walking.
- Multiplayer loot handoff: live DB is off limits.
- The Town in-page Realm overlay (other lanes).
