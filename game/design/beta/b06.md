# Beta b06: isles exploration, biomes, bosses, travel, weather/env

Branch `beta-b06`, port 9455. About 40 minutes of wall-clock, heavily queued behind the shared 5-slot browser semaphore (load average 12-14), so I got roughly 10 scripted sessions instead of a long hand-played run. All contexts used `blockDb`: 0 DB requests, 0 page errors, 0 console errors in every session.

## Covered
- All five isles (home/meadow, jungle, tundra, reef, mars) at 390x844 touch: spawn screen, HUD, creature density, every pack species spawned and screenshotted (`src` png vs code checked), boss site per island (fernjaw_tyrant, tide_hydra, frost_dragon, red_titan) spawned with its arena, telegraph and boss bar.
- URL travel without `test=1`: `?isle=reef` / `mars` land at the Hearth dock with the right "opens with a raft / rocket core" toast; `ice`, `home`, `tundra`, `jungle`, `bogus`, `__proto__` all resolve safely. With `test=1` the direct load works and `travel('reef'|'mars')` from the dock returns false while locked.
- Sea chart UI at the dock (phone): lists open islands, locked rows with unlock cost and "Craft it". Crafted raft + rocket_core_drift, tapped "Sail to Red Waste": lift-off scene, arrival on the mars landing pad, unlock list updated.
- Boss kill and weary on the jungle tyrant (weary at 10 percent, then dead on extra damage).
- Death and respawn: lethal hit toasts "You wash up on the beach.", respawns at spawn point with full HP (real-time, works).
- Env meters: warmth drains (6/s on the model, 90 s) and the hot spring refills it; mars air shows "Air 90 s".

## Not reached (gaps)
- Hand-played real-time combat on every island, boss kills by dodging (the unit tests cover this), the rematch pedestal fight (all `can:false` on a fresh save; no trophies in that save), trophies placement.
- Thin ice (`ice_thin` terrain; my probe used the wrong name), aurora at night, hot-spring look, mars air empty damage: the hero starts in a sheltered tile on tundra and mars (dock / habitat) which refills the meter, and I ran out of queue time to walk out of it. The meter numbers I saw were correct, the damage branch is untested by me.
- Landscape 844x390 and desktop 1280x720 HUD, boat/dock soft-lock hunting by walking, stuck-on-rocks checks.
- Feast boss-tame completion: blocked by finding 04 in my sequence (I killed the weary boss); not retried.

1. b06-03 Frost Dragon and Red Titan are the same recolored yeti-like sprite; no dragon, weakest art at two climaxes.
2. b06-02 Mars: all five species are code-drawn from two shared templates (blob, quadruped); names do not match looks.
3. b06-01 Tundra: 4 of 7 species are code-drawn generics (icicle_bat is not a bat) beside nice PNG penguin/fox/wolf.
4. b06-04 A weary boss dies after about 4 s of held fire; tame is easy to lose accidentally.
5. b06-05 Home, jungle and tundra spawn screens are empty (0 creatures at start, about 1 after a minute); reef has 9-12.
6. b06-09 The first-visit how-to card hides the centre of the screen and bosses; toasts overprint it.
7. b06-07 HUD island name is truncated on every island at 390 wide.
8. b06-06 Spawn areas look like flat single-tone fields on jungle, reef and mars.
9. b06-08 Rocket lift-off is drawn under the toast and the bottom buttons.

- Boss telegraphs (cone, line, ring) are very readable, with clear boss bar and "Step out of the glowing shape" hint; the jungle ruin arena floor looks good.
- Sea chart is clean: unlock cost with have/need, "Craft it" next to every locked island, URL-locking handled gracefully with an explanatory toast.
- Death is gentle (full HP, toast) and travel/unlock chain worked first time.
