# Beta b03: dungeons, mutators, deep runs, bosses, Trial of Six

Tester b03, branch `beta-b03`, ~60 min (05:26-06:10 UTC). Playwright Chromium headless, 1280x720 desktop and 390x844 touch phone. Database blocked
(`blockDb`, 0 DB requests in every run). No page errors or console errors in any run.

## What I covered
- All four biomes via URL (`?mode=dungeon&biome=meadow|crypt|warren|bramble`): start room, room flow, every non-secret room on floor 1-3 (warren and
  bramble on phone, meadow and crypt on desktop). No unreachable room, no enemy inside a wall, no hero inside a wall on entry, no stuck sealed door.
- Real keyboard and mouse play with a scripted player (WASD + aim + hold fire, path to doors, keys, chests). Door entry checked at different offsets.
- Mutators: chips seen for Swift, Swarm, Mirror, Dim, Brittle, Narrow (meadow/crypt). Warren and bramble show no chip, as designed. Floor chip "✦ Brittle · Floor 2" is correct.
- Deep runs: boss kill, then "Floor 1 of 3 cleared" card (Stairs / Leave with your loot), stairs to floor 2 and 3, portal on floor 3, summary (Floor 3 of 3, loot list, Lantern stash line).
- Bosses: Mire Queen, Lich King, Bone Colossus, Queen Burrowmole (boss bars, phases, defeat banner, two bags each).
- Secret walls (crack tile, bolt shooting), locked door "Locked — find the key".
- Dungeon fall with real damage (walked into the Lich King), the fall summary, its buttons and the R key.
- Trial of Six: Lv 10 gate check (from existing tests), Lv 25 seeded save, walk into the Nexus, gate prompt, the Inferno Wyrm fight with real input on desktop and phone, a fall in the trial, Esc during the trial.

## Coverage gaps (not reached)
- Trial steps 2-6 and the Next / Rest / Leave panel with real input: my Lv 25 hero had only the starter wand and died to boss 1 within 10-40 s each time. Not enough time to seed a geared save.
- Realm portals to dungeons from the world (I used URLs and the Nexus only), pity counter values, relic drops.
- Telegraph timing of each boss (I only saw them in screenshots), landscape phone, video clips.

## Top findings
1. **b03-01 (P1)** In the Trial of Six, Enter (which the HUD lists as a fire key) opens the Nexus over the live boss fight. The boss spawns at the Nexus portal, so the "Visit the Nexus (Enter)" prompt stays up under the hero. `realm-world.js:1987` has no rush check.
2. **b03-02 (P2)** Doors are one tile wide with no corner slip: 9 px or more off the centre line, holding the direction stops the hero on the frame. Measured at W, E and N doors.
3. **b03-07 (P2)** `?floor=3` works straight from the URL. A fresh Lv 1 save gets T10/T11 floor-3 boss loot without clearing floors 1-2.
4. **b03-03 (P2)** The "New best tier" loot toast lands exactly on the boss name and HP bar at the start of boss fights. On phones it also stacks with the minimap and the "defeated" banner.
5. **b03-04 (P2)** Phone boss fight: the boss bar overlaps the minimap (right end) and the mutator chip (left end). The rectangles are measured in the JSON.
6. **b03-05 (P2)** Phone portrait: the 2x2 ability grid and the sticks cover the lower third of the room. About 100 px of HUD is stacked above the room.
7. **b03-06 (P2)** Desktop: the Q/E/R/F bar hides the bottom-centre door, the hero entering through it, and secret cracks on that wall.
8. **b03-08 (P3)** The boss bar hides the hero who enters a boss room from the N door.
9. **b03-09 / b03-10 (P3)** The Trial is fought in the Safe-ish starter zone, with world mobs and a raid timer around it. A fall shows only the generic world fall line, with no Trial result.
10. **b03-13 (P2)** On the dungeon fall summary, R (the universal Restart key) does nothing, and the card has no same-seed retry. Before the card appears, the world banner ("You fell in the Realm… wave 1") shows for about 2.5 s (b03-14).
11. **b03-12 (P3)** Boss HP is the same on floors 2 and 3. Crypt floor 2 (Bone Colossus 260) was easier than floor 1 (Lich King 612).

## What felt great
- Room flow is solid. In every room I tried, sealed doors opened on clear, keys and locks worked, the minimap filled in, and the HUD line ("Crypt · Room 6/7 · 🗝 1 · Ossuary") was clear.
- The deep-run card is calm and readable on phones and has no timer. The run summary (rooms, keys, secret, mutator, floor, loot, stash) is clear.
- Boss art (Mire Queen, Lich King, Bone Colossus, Inferno Wyrm) is strong, and each biome has its own palette.
- No errors, no DB traffic, and no soft-locks in about 15 full dungeon floors.

## Readiness for my lane: 7/10
The core dungeon loop is ready. The Trial of Six needs the Enter/Nexus fix and its own fight space. Phone HUD stacking during bosses needs a pass.

## Could not test and why
- Live multiplayer / co-op dungeons: not my lane, and the database stays blocked by rule.
- Clips: no P0 found. The P1 is easy to reproduce from the steps and needs no clip.
- Run time: about 4 of 5 browser slots were busy with other testers, and several runs waited minutes for a slot.
