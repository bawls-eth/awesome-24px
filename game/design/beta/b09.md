# Beta report b09: Arcade A (Pixel Pop, Lantern Dash, Stack Tower, Echo Garden)

Branch beta-b09, port 9458, about 35 min wall clock (05:26 to 06:02 UTC; the shared browser semaphore starved several queued runs, so fewer real-time sessions than planned).

## Covered
- Pixel Pop: phone real-touch 90 s round with a greedy tapper, desktop test-mode stress (60 seeds x 80 taps, 4800 taps) of merge/chain/Big pop/shuffle, seed case handling and Replay seed, medals and two-tab save merge, reload persistence, keyboard play, desktop resize, result card on phone and desktop.
- Lantern Dash: real-time phone mashing run, pause on blur (freezes, resume tap is not a jump), landscape 844x390, ghost replay determinism on 6 seeds (first 217 frames compared, no mismatch), result card.
- Stack Tower: overhang math over 12 drops with chosen offsets (cut, snap, widen +10 at combo 3, cap 220, flush perfect), share code and seed round trip, timeout result, two-tab merge, real-time human-like bot (11 floors), phone intro/result.
- Echo Garden: sequence rules (seed case-insensitive, no triple repeats), perfect play to a 12-note tune (medals t6, clean8, t10 awarded), wrong tap (leaf lost, slower replay, progress resets), third wilt ends, tap during show ignored, real touch input, cues log (bloom_*, error, claim).
- Zero console or page errors in any run; Firebase blocked in every context.

## Not reached / could not test
- Audio itself (headless). Only Pop `lastSound` and Echo `cues()` can be read: Pop chain ladder (combo5 at chain 5) and Echo bloom_c/d/e/g, error, claim, levelup are correct. Dash and Stack expose no cue log (b09-11).
- Dash ghost compare only covers the first 217 frames (the idle player died); a long survived-run ghost race was not recorded. The code path looks consistent.
- No video clips (no P0/P1). No long real-time sessions, no 150% text or low-gfx pass (covered by the earlier bug bash notes), no `?seed=` share link across two devices.

## Top findings
1. b09-02 (P2): Pop and Echo result cards have no tap guard. On a phone the card covers the whole board, so a mashing finger at the buzzer hits Play again or Replay seed and skips the results. Stack already has a 450 ms click guard; Dash has a 700 ms re-arm. Share the guard in MMMiniKit.
2. b09-01 (P2): Pop score wraps to two lines from 5 digits on 390 px, which happens within a minute.
3. b09-03 (P2): Echo score popup is drawn over the flower labels and the centre tune/leaves widget.
4. b09-04 (P3): Pop chain preview/hint can over-promise (2.4% of taps the real chain was shorter than predicted; the code comment says it cannot be).
5. b09-10 (P3): Pop is too easy to medal: three of four medals in the first round for a greedy tapper, 76k points. Little one-more-try pull after that; the +5 s bonus is wasted above 85 s.
6. b09-06 (P3): Stack Perfect window is 5 px but the swing reaches 340 px/s (5.7 px per frame) after floor 36, so late Perfects and the 40 floors medal become luck.
7. b09-07 (P3): Dash keeps one ghost; a short run on a New course erases the ghost of your best course.
8. b09-08 (P3): Dash result card sits below the fold on a phone.
9. b09-05 / b09-09 (P3): Stack stacked near-miss texts garble; intro/result overlay covers header and Sound button on phone.
10. b09-11 (P3): `__dash.course` getter shadowed; no cue log in Dash/Stack.

## What felt great
- Overhang math in Stack is exactly right; snap, widen and the width cap behave as documented, and the "N px from perfect" and near-best lines are a good retry hook.
- Echo is calm and fair: no triple repeats, forgiving wilt with slower replay, number plus shape plus colour on every flower, perfect run feels rewarding, medals line up with progress.
- Pop merge rules are sound (flood, big pop +2, chain loop, shuffle on no moves never left a dead board in 4800 taps), seeds deterministic and case-insensitive, hover preview is a good teaching aid.
- Two-tab saves merge cleanly (best never lowered, rounds and medals kept); reload keeps medals and best; hostile case handling fine.
- Dash pause on blur is clean and the resume tap does not jump; ghost replay matched the recorded run frame for frame.
- Shared result card (stats, best flag, share code, copy, goal line) is clear and consistent across the four games.

## Readiness score (area): 8 / 10
No crash, soft-lock or data loss. Remaining issues are polish (HUD wrap, overlapping popups, missing tap guard) and balance tuning.
