# Beta b08: new-player journey, flow, navigation, progression

Tester b08, branch `beta-b08`, tip `ae30c26`, 2026-11-23 05:26-06:10 UTC (about 45 min of play). Playwright, phone 390x844
(touch, isMobile, `navigator.webdriver` masked so the root-URL front door runs like a real browser), plus one desktop 1280x720
look. Firebase blocked in every context (route + routeWebSocket); the Town was played through its own "Play offline".

## What I covered
- Fresh phone player at the plain root `/game/`: landing, first move, time to the first screen of each pillar.
- Every pillar from the Home grid: Town (join card, offline town), The Realm (hero pick, coach cards 1-3, a death), Isles, Lantern Camp,
  Pixel Pop, Angler, Stack Tower (a full round). Menu "Where to?" on each, phone Back (history) from each.
- Progress feeding Home: tokens/medals/Continue/Next after a Stack round; Collections sheet; Daily gift; Map toggle and Open; Settings from the Menu.
- Returning player (saved storage from the first session) at the root URL again: Continue memory, Realm hero showing in Lantern Camp, Back from Lantern.

Isles taming, Realm dungeon/Nexus steps 4-6, Lantern Dash, Echo, Jam, Bug Safari, Stalk, Deepdelve not played past their first screen.
Live Town (multiplayer) not tested by rule. Landscape rotation not done. No long-save (weeks of progress) fixture.

## Flow, as a new player
1. Root URL lands on a good-looking 8-bit Home in ~2 s: big orange PLAY, then 13 place cards in three groups, Collections, a footer. No scroll on a phone. Clear and inviting.
2. The first move is NOT obvious: PLAY says "Start in the Town", the line under it says "Next: Make a first friend (Isles)", and the coached adventure (Realm) is just a card. PLAY opens a form (name, 10 portraits, live server). Offline it is ~13 s before "Play offline" appears; I reached the town at 21 s.
3. The Realm is the best first five minutes (portrait pick, then "Drag the left stick" 1/6, "Drag the right stick" 2/6, which both advance on the right action, and first XP within 15 s). Then card 3 "Walk over a loot bag" gave me no bag or pointer, an event timer ran from spawn ("Raid in 0:08"), and I died at 44 s.
4. Arcade: Stack Tower is fun within 10 s. Home noticed the round straight away (tokens 0 to 10, medals 0 to 1, PLAY became "CONTINUE Stack Tower", Next became "15 floors (Stack Tower)"). That loop works well.
5. Menu "Where to?" (compass, top-left) is on every page I opened except the Town join card. In the Town it is a different button ("Where to?", top-right). Its first row is a correct contextual Back ("Back to Home", "Back to The Realm"), and Settings is at the bottom.
6. Phone Back returned to Home from every card I tried, and from Lantern back to the Realm. Continue remembered the last place across sessions.
7. Dead ends: Daily gift Claim ("Nothing was claimed"), Map > Open ("Coming soon"), and the Town join card (no Menu).

## Top 10 flow improvements (ranked)
1. **Make the Daily gift claimable on any save, or hide Claim until the Town save exists** (b08-01, P1). Right now the first gift a new player sees is a broken button.
2. **One first call to action.** PLAY should go into the coached Realm shore for a first run (SIMPLIFY section 6), and "Next" should point at that same place, not the Isles (b08-02).
3. **Make the tutorial shore safe:** no event countdown and no deaths during coach cards 1-4, and drop a visible bag or show an arrow at step 3 (b08-04).
4. **Put the compass Menu on the Town join card and in the Town**, top-left, and use one wording everywhere ("Back to Home") (b08-03, b08-06).
5. **Map: start the player on a real place** (or make Open enter it), and use the Home names on the map (Town, Lantern Camp, Angler, Isles) (b08-05).
6. **End cards: Again + Menu** on every arcade game (Stack has no Menu button) (b08-07).
7. **Strip key names and text walls on touch:** keep at most 25 words before Play, and show stick/A/B glyphs (Isles, Pop, Stack, Town join card, Settings Help) (b08-08).
8. **Show 3 arcade games first and reveal the rest one at a time.** Fold Lantern Camp into the Adventure as its "camp" screen, so one hero creator (b08-09).
9. **Clean the Home footer:** drop "← Town" (it duplicates PLAY) and "Open" on the Home view. Fit Collections above the fold on desktop (b08-10).
10. **Town phone HUD diet:** 3 numbers, Primary and Secondary buttons at 56 px or more, emotes behind a button, no walkie while offline. Offer "Play offline" at once beside Join, and say "Type a name first" on the disabled Join (b08-06, b08-11). One Menu button in the Realm, and nothing between the sticks (b08-12).

## What felt great
- The Home screen: 8-bit logo, sky, readable cards with medal pips, no scroll at 390x844, consistent compass Menu in all the mini games.
- Progress feedback after one arcade round (tokens, medal, Continue, Next) is immediate and correct, and survives a new session.
- The Realm coach cards use touch wording ("Drag the left stick") and advance on real actions. The portrait hero picker is clear.
- The hero made in the Realm shows up in Lantern Camp (one hero roster).
- Settings is reachable from the Menu on the Hub, and the sheet is clean and large.
- Zero JS errors on any page I opened (the only console errors were the blocked database requests), and no horizontal scroll.

## Score
Flow and navigation readiness: **6/10**. The skeleton (Home, Menu, Continue, Back, progress feeding Home) is solid. The first five
minutes are not yet one guided path: three competing first moves, a broken first gift, a tutorial you can die in, and a Town that
looks and controls like a different game.
