# Beta b13 - Performance and stability (branch beta-b13, 2026-11-23, 05:26-06:25 UTC)

## Method and honest caveats
- Chromium headless, software rendering (no GPU), Playwright + CDP: `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate` 4x and 6x, `Network.emulateNetworkConditions` (Fast-3G 200 KB/s/150 ms, Slow-3G 50 KB/s/400 ms), `Performance.getMetrics` (Nodes, JSEventListeners), `Runtime.getHeapUsage` after `HeapProfiler.collectGarbage`, rAF frame deltas, PerformanceObserver longtask, AudioContext/addEventListener wrappers. Firebase blocked in every context (blockDb + SDK stub, Town via "Play offline" with ADMIN name); nothing was sent to the live DB.
- **The box was at load average 20-24 for the whole session (20 agents, 4 cores).** Absolute fps and long-task numbers are therefore pessimistic and noisy; counts (requests, bytes, DOM nodes, listeners, heap, textures, tweens) are not affected and are the reliable part. `performance.memory` is quantised to 9.5 MB in headless, so heap numbers come from CDP only (soak-town/gen runs; the first Realm soak printed the quantised value, its leak evidence is Nodes/JSEventListeners).
- The 17-page survey drives pages with random keys and (on phone) random taps for 6 s after a 3 s settle, mostly on their title/menu states; it is a load/idle/light-input matrix, not a full fight. Heavy-play numbers are in the soak sections. Random taps on 390 px often hit the nav sheet and navigate away; two "leaks" I first saw (echo, arena: nodes 500 -> 1600, listeners 30/370 -> 2000) were exactly that (page ended on the Realm: same ~2000 listeners), verified with a listener-stack probe. They are not leaks.

## Coverage (about 60 min)
Survey 17 pages x 2 viewports at 4x (34 runs) + 6x on 14 pages + Slow/Fast-3G on Realm, Town, Isles; two soaks (Realm fight 10 min at 4x; Town offline with 55 remote players 8 min at 4x with churn/bubbles); Town A/B 0 vs 55 remotes at 1x and 4x on both viewports; Deepdelve view-distance/governor runs at 1x/4x on both viewports; resize storm (60 rotations), visibility hide/show, 5 reloads (audio contexts, heap, nodes) on Realm, Dungeon, Isles, Deepdelve, Hub, Dash; 150 s random-input soaks on 9 mini/dungeon pages; existing `world-perf.test.js` at the game's own 4x gate (passes: median 16.8 ms, p95 183 ms, worst 567 ms, 15 of 126 frames > 100 ms with 60 enemies + 300 bullets; update+draw 3.9-4.8 ms per frame in the 30-enemy gear case).

## Top findings (prose)
1. b13-01 P1: the Realm/Arena/Dungeon art loader refetches the same INDEX.json (7x) and pack manifests (up to 5x) because `fetchJson` in `game/shared/mm-art.js` has no cache; 1,235 requests / 8 MB on a cold Realm start, 2.6 MB duplicate. On Slow-3G the Realm needs 153 s to finish loading and 33 s to be usable.
2. b13-03 P1: Town with the capped 55 remote players is 9-12 fps at 4x (and ~12 fps idle at 4x here); cost is render/fill, not JS (script 7-19% of main thread). Needs a real-device check since software GL exaggerates it, but p95 doubling at 1x when the crowd appears is real.
3. b13-05 P2: Deepdelve start-up (0.9-1.0 s frames at 4x) and tap-edits (up to 0.8 s at 1x) are the worst main-thread blocks I measured; the quality governor does not react because the spikes are short.
4. b13-04 P2: Realm fight frame time worsens over 10 minutes (55 -> 35 fps, long tasks x6) while all object counts are flat; possible hot-path/GC creep, confounded by box load; please re-run on a quiet machine.
5. b13-06 P2: Isles needs 3-5 s to DOMContentLoaded under 4-6x CPU.
6. b13-02 P2: Town and Hub load 654 KB of three.js only the underground needs, with a deprecation warning that says this file disappears after r160 (the pin is r160).
7. b13-07 P3: rotate storms block 350-450 ms and leave the Realm at 0.75 render scale until reload.
8. b13-08 P3: Realm listeners creep +60 per 10 min.
9. b13-09 P3 favicon 404 on Arcane Frontier; b13-10 P3 ~15 MB of unreferenced large PNGs in the deploy folder; b13-11 P3 art URLs are unversioned.

## What looked healthy
No leak found anywhere I could measure: Town (8 min, 55 remotes with churn), Realm (10 min fight), Isles/Dungeon/Dash/Stack/Jam (150 s each), 5-reload cycles, resize storms. Mini games (Dash, Echo, Angler, Pop, Stack, Jam, Stalk, Bugs, Lantern) are light: <= 0.31 MB, <= 25 requests, 45-60 fps even at 6x. Pool sizes plateau (72/700/160). World-perf gate in the repo passes. Audio contexts do not pile up. Hidden-tab and reload paths are clean.

## Readiness score (performance and stability): 7/10
Memory/stability is good (9/10). Cold-start weight of the Realm family and phone performance of Town crowds / Deepdelve are the weak spots.

## Not tested, and why
- No real GPU or real phone: all fps are software-rendered and the box was at load 20+; recommend re-measuring b13-03/04/05 on a quiet machine or a device.
- Isles with a party / Isles boss and dungeon rooms with many enemies were only soaked with light random input (no party stub, no scripted combat there); Deepdelve 10 min soak and Isles 10 min soak not done (time + 5-slot semaphore queue; the 150 s soaks showed flat counts).
- Mini-game long soaks used 150 s, not 10 min; localStorage growth was checked on 5 mini games (110 key presses + 3 reloads each): bounded, no key grows after its first write (Dash 206 -> 798 chars total = first-write of dash/profile/medals keys, then flat; Jam 167, Pop 192, Stack 616, Echo 611 flat), no errors.
- Live-multiplayer behaviour (real remote traffic, Firebase listener counts) is out of bounds by the brief.
- Raw data scripts live outside the repo (scratchpad); numbers above are copied verbatim from their JSON output.
